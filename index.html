About Us

OURA is a clothing brand that is both fashion-forward and environmentally conscious. Its name represents “Our Era” and “Aura,” reflecting our generation and the unique energy each person carries.

Our logo combines the letters of OURA, with the letters shaped like two people hugging, symbolizing connection, comfort, and acceptance. Inspired by nature, we create timeless clothing using sustainable materials.
